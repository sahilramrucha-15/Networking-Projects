Vlans were configured on the L3 and L2 Switch

VLAN 10 - Finance
VLAN 20 - Engineering
VLAN 30 - HR
VLAN 40 - Servers
VLAN 99 - Network Management

Intervlaning will be preformed by the L3 Switch, therefore SVI were created for each vlan

VLAN 10 - 192.168.5.2/26
VLAN 20 - 192.168.5.65/26
VLAN 30 - 192.168.5.129/26
VLAN 40 - 192.168.5.193/29
VLAN 99 - 192.168.5.206/29


Router's g0/0 interface was configured with 192.168.5.1/26
File server - 192.168.5.194/29
DHCP server - 192.168.5.195/29

Trunk ports were assigned on SW1 Fa0/24 and SW2 Fa0/24 with 802.1q encapsulation.

DHCP server was configured with DHCP pools

VLAN 10 - 192.168.5.3 - 192.168.5.63
VLAN 20 - 192.168.5.66 - 192.168.5.127
VLAN 30 - 192.168.5.129 - 192.168.5.190


The DHCP is configured on VLAN 40, the IP helper address command was added to
each SVI, to turn the L3 switch to a DHCP relay agent.

Interface fa0/1 on L3 was made into a L3 routed interface  192.168.5.202/30
Gateway of last resort on L3 switch was configured as 192.168.5.201/30
Static routes were configured on the Router.

192.168.5.0/26 via 192.168.5.210/30
192.168.5.64/26 via 192.168.5.210/30
192.168.5.128/26 via 192.168.5.210/30
192.168.5.192/29 via 192.168.5.210/30
192.168.5.200/29 via 192.168.5.210/30

SSH was configured on the L3, L2 and Router.

ACLs were configured to prevent VLANs from communication with each other.They are able to access the servers.

Portfast and bpdu Guard was implemeted on SW1 fa0/1-fa0/10 and SW2 fa0/2 - fa0/10 (Ports connected to end devices)

Standard ACLs were configured to disallow PCs not in VLAN 99 from trying to create TCP sessions with switches and routers.
